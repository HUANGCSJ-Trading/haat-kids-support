Privacy policy

HAAT kids: little steps · Effective September 19, 2026

Data used inside the app

The app stores child profile names, selected icons, routines, missions, star balances, completion history, reward redemption records, and preferences locally on your device. You may use a nickname. We do not receive this family data, operate a server to store it, or sell or share it.

No tracking or advertising

The app does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not require an account, collect location, access your contacts, or record microphone audio. Spoken prompts use Apple's device speech features, not an external AI voice service.

Storage, backups, and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it using available app controls or delete the app and its data from your device. Device backups may contain app data depending on your Apple settings. We cannot access, restore, or delete copies held in your device backups. Manage those backups through Apple.

Children

The app is intended for family use with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app. A parent or guardian should handle support requests and avoid including identifying information about a child.

If you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and troubleshoot the problem, retain it only as needed for support or applicable obligations, and do not use it for advertising. You can request deletion of support correspondence at huangcsj@icloud.com, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Apple services and this website

App Store purchases, device backups, and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple are handled under Apple's policies and settings. This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to provide and secure its hosting service. This site adds no analytics or advertising scripts. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if the app's privacy practices change. Contact HAAT kids at huangcsj@icloud.com for privacy questions.
