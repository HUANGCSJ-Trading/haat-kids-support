Piko Pet Support

Piko Pet is designed as a gentle first virtual-pet experience. Feed, cuddle, bathe, brush, rest, dance, or play ball with a pet. Tap PLAY for Star Hop, Bubble Pop, Memory Match, Snack Sort, and Garden Maze.

  • Progress: Pet rooms, names, growth, and stars are saved on the device.
  • Languages: English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, and German.
  • Reassurance: Pets never become sick or disappear while a child is away. They simply rest and wait.
  • Device: Piko Pet is made for iPhone and requires iOS 16 or later.

Need help? Email huangcsj@icloud.com. Please include the app name, iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send a child’s name or other personal information.

Piko Pet Privacy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

HAAT Kids: Piko Pet is made for children and is designed to work offline. The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data through the app.

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, in-app purchases, subscriptions, analytics, or tracking.
  • No chat, user-generated content, external web content, or social features.
  • No access to location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • Pet names, progress, settings, rooms, and stars stay locally on the device and may be included in the device owner’s normal Apple backup.

If a parent or guardian contacts support by email, the information they choose to provide is used only to reply and resolve the request. Please do not include a child’s personal information.

Questions about this policy may be sent to huangcsj@icloud.com.

Little Steps Privacy

Little Steps is designed with children and families in mind. The app does not collect or transmit personal data, require an account, include advertising, or track users. Routine progress and settings are stored locally on the device and may be included in the device owner’s normal Apple backup.

If a parent or guardian contacts support by email, the information they choose to provide is used only to reply and resolve the request. Please do not include a child’s personal information.
